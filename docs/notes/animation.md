# Animation and the first-person view

- **No Anim Blueprint.** `UVelisectAnimInstance` is native; its proxy overrides `Evaluate` (as `UAnimSingleNodeInstance` does). Gameplay calls `Play(Layer, Anim, Rate, bLoop, BlendTime, bRestart)`; channels cross-fade by weight. Negative rates play backwards (`FVelisectLocoClip.NaturalSpeed < 0`).
- **Layers:** `Base` drives the whole body (locomotion, jump, death); `Upper` drives `spine` and its subtree with alpha. The upper root's rotation blends in **component space**, so the torso keeps the clip's facing over any hips (without it the rifle pointed ~45° left).
- **Aim:** `AimRotation` (component space) is split evenly over spine, spine1, spine2: view pitch × `AimPitchScale` (−1 on this rig) + `AimPitchOffset` 6.4, yaw trim `AimYawOffset` 12. Measured: muzzle within 0.3° of the view at −35°…+30°.
- **Player clips:** aim pose = `A_Player_FiringRifle` frozen at frame 0 (also the hold while firing: `bProceduralFire`, spine kick 2°/shot); low ready in sprint = `RifleTurn` frame 0; idle legs = `RifleTurn` frame 0; reload `Reloading` ×1.35; equip `RiflePullOut` ×1.4 at spawn; hit `HitReaction`; death `Dying`; jump `Jump` ×1.4; crouch idle = `CrouchStopWalking` held at its end. Move clips by direction F, FR, R, BR, B, BL, L, FL: `SprintForward` 714, `SprintForwardRight` 716, `StrafeRight` 108, `RunBackwardRight` 475, `SprintForward` reversed, `RunBackwardLeft`, `StrafeLeft`, `SprintForwardLeft` (cm/s natural speeds, rate clamped 0.35–1.6). Crouch: `CrouchRunning` 130 (reversed for B), `WalkCrouchingRight/Left` 202.
- **Zombie clips:** idle, walk (34 cm/s, shambles at wander 60), run (304; chase 300), scream ×1.2 on first sight, attack/biting ×1.5 (capped 2.2 s), death/dying. Unused: FastRun, crawl, running crawl, neck bite, biting 2.
- **Viewmodel (M4 framing, M5 takes):** the owner sees `FirstPersonArms` (SK_PlayerArms: Ch35 cut to arm and hand faces by `export_arms.py`, same skeleton) on the camera with `SK_RifleFP` in its right hand (`ViewmodelGripOffset`); both are UE first-person primitives (FOV 70, scale 0.6); the body and `WorldWeapon` (SM_Rifle) are the world representation. The arms play authored takes on their Base layer only (no upper layer, spine aim or IK) and the rifle plays the matching `A_RifleFP_*` take (bones `magazine`, `magazine_spare`, `bolt`, `trigger`), both through `PlayViewmodel`: `Idle` (2 s breathing loop; its first frame is the hold), `Equip` 0.6 s, `ReloadTac` 2.0 s (rounds left), `ReloadEmpty` 2.73 s (adds the rack), and `A_RifleFP_Fire` (bolt cycle) per shot. The body's Mixamo `Reloading` is stretched to the same length for the shadow. Console: `VsReload`, `VsReloadEmpty`.
- **How the takes are made (`export_fp.py`, Blender):** Blender world = Unreal component space with Y flipped (checked exactly on bones). The rifle sits at `viewmodel.hold.rifle` (arms space, the M4 solve's result). Hands: a palm frame from the rest pose placed on each grip (the pistol grip by the index knuckle so the finger reaches the trigger; foregrip and magazine by the palm), two-bone IK with the elbow bending in its hinge plane and half the wrist twist on the forearm. Fingers: each finger's curl is searched for the pose that hugs the surface without entering it (BVH of the grip); thumbs and the trigger finger are aimed first. Presets: trigger, safe (finger straight along the receiver), fore, mag, paddle (thumb on the release), handle, open. Tracks: the rifle as an eye-space offset turned about the grip (roll about its own bore); magazine, spare and left hand keyed in rifle, eye or part space and Hermite-interpolated, each key evaluated at the current time in its own space (a hand keyed on the magazine follows it); the tossed magazine falls ballistically. Check without Unreal: `VS_FP=hold`, `VS_FP=<Clip>` (contact sheet every 6 frames) or `VS_FP=<Clip>:20+30` (full frames) render to `Saved/ToolLogs/fp/`. In game: `pie_seq.py` with `slomo 0.2;VsReload`.
- **Reload choreography (AK):** rifle up, in and canted 30 deg; thumb on the paddle, rock the magazine out about its front lug and toss it; fresh magazine from below the view, front lug hooked at 28 deg, rocked in (click jolt); hand back to the foregrip. Empty: the rifle then rolls onto its left side (-75 deg) and the left hand racks the handle over the top.
- **Left hand on the body:** `FVelisectLeftHandIK` still holds the body's world rifle (mirrored right-hand grip); the first-person arms no longer use it.
- **Motion:** kick (back 1 cm per degree), look sway (0.35, max 2.5 deg), bob, and the sprint hold (arms keep aiming; the rifle tilts -8/-14/-16 deg and drops) turn the viewmodel about the right hand in `UpdateViewmodel`. The body still bends its spine to aim (`BodyOffset`, `AimPitch*`) for its shadow.
- **Legs** are out of view when looking down (body sits behind the camera): accepted for now.
