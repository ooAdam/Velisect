# Animation and the first-person view

- **No Anim Blueprint.** `UVelisectAnimInstance` is native; its proxy overrides `Evaluate` (as `UAnimSingleNodeInstance` does). Gameplay calls `Play(Layer, Anim, Rate, bLoop, BlendTime, bRestart)`; channels cross-fade by weight. Negative rates play backwards (`FVelisectLocoClip.NaturalSpeed < 0`).
- **Layers:** `Base` drives the whole body (locomotion, jump, death); `Upper` drives `spine` and its subtree with alpha. The upper root's rotation blends in **component space**, so the torso keeps the clip's facing over any hips (without it the rifle pointed ~45° left).
- **Aim:** `AimRotation` (component space) is split evenly over spine, spine1, spine2: view pitch × `AimPitchScale` (−1 on this rig) + `AimPitchOffset` 6.4, yaw trim `AimYawOffset` 12. Measured: muzzle within 0.3° of the view at −35°…+30°.
- **Player clips:** aim pose = `A_Player_FiringRifle` frozen at frame 0 (also the hold while firing: `bProceduralFire`, spine kick 2°/shot); low ready in sprint = `RifleTurn` frame 0; idle legs = `RifleTurn` frame 0; reload `Reloading` ×1.35; equip `RiflePullOut` ×1.4 at spawn; hit `HitReaction`; death `Dying`; jump `Jump` ×1.4; crouch idle = `CrouchStopWalking` held at its end. Move clips by direction F, FR, R, BR, B, BL, L, FL: `SprintForward` 714, `SprintForwardRight` 716, `StrafeRight` 108, `RunBackwardRight` 475, `SprintForward` reversed, `RunBackwardLeft`, `StrafeLeft`, `SprintForwardLeft` (cm/s natural speeds, rate clamped 0.35–1.6). Crouch: `CrouchRunning` 130 (reversed for B), `WalkCrouchingRight/Left` 202.
- **Zombie clips:** idle, walk (34 cm/s, shambles at wander 60), run (304; chase 300), scream ×1.2 on first sight, attack/biting ×1.5 (capped 2.2 s), death/dying. Unused: FastRun, crawl, running crawl, neck bite, biting 2.
- **Viewmodel (M4, Valorant framing):** the owner sees `FirstPersonArms` (SK_PlayerArms: Ch35 cut to arm and hand faces by `export_arms.py`, same skeleton) on the camera, with the rifle in its right hand. Both are UE first-person primitives (FOV 70, scale 0.6, so they never poke through walls); the body and `WorldWeapon` are the world representation (shadow, death cam). The arms run their own `UVelisectAnimInstance`: base = aim pose, the upper-body clips over it (`PlayUpper` drives both), no spine aim. `ue_tune_player.py` frames the rifle from the user's reference: `viewmodel.frame` pins muzzle, front sight and scope rear to screen spots; it searches the rifle's yaw/pitch, solves its position, and sets `ViewmodelOffset` from the aim pose's right hand (AnimPose API). Result: rifle 61 cm ahead, 16 right, 30 down, yawed 8.5 deg in, pitched 6 deg up.
- **Left hand:** `FVelisectLeftHandIK` in the proxy holds the vertical foregrip the way the right hand holds the pistol grip, mirrored across the rifle (this rig mirrors left/right as quaternion (x, -y, -z, w)), fingers included; two-bone IK with an elbow pole hint (0, 0, -70) so the forearm rises from below. Off during reload and equip.
- **Motion:** kick (back 1 cm per degree), look sway (0.35, max 2.5 deg), bob, and the sprint hold (arms keep aiming; the rifle tilts -8/-14/-16 deg and drops) turn the viewmodel about the right hand in `UpdateViewmodel`. The body still bends its spine to aim (`BodyOffset`, `AimPitch*`) for its shadow.
- **Legs** are out of view when looking down (body sits behind the camera): accepted for now.
